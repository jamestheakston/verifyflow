Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 22, 2025

VerifyFlow ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our email verification service ("Service").

By using our Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect various types of information to provide and improve our Service to you.

1.1. Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect information such as your email address and a password. If you use Google Sign-In, we receive your email address and public profile information (like your display name and profile picture URL) from Google.
  • Profile Information: You may choose to provide a display name and a profile picture URL in your dashboard settings.
  • Contact Information: If you contact us for support or inquiries, we will collect the information you provide, such as your name and email address.
  • Webhook Configuration Data: When you configure our webhook, you provide us with:
    • to_address: The email address of the user you wish to verify.
    • reply_to (optional): An email address for replies to the verification email.
    • app_name: The name of your application, used in the verification email.
    • redirect_url: The URL where the user will be redirected after successful verification.

1.2. Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data: We may collect information on how the Service is accessed and used. This Usage Data may include information such as your computer's Internet Protocol address (e.g., IP address), browser type, browser version, the pages of our Service that you visit, the time and date of your visit, the time spent on those pages, unique device identifiers, and other diagnostic data.
  • Cookies and Tracking Technologies: We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to track activity on our Service and hold certain information.
    • Cookies: Small files placed on your device. You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. However, if you do not accept cookies, you may not be able to use some portions of our Service.
    • Firebase Authentication Cookies: Firebase Authentication uses cookies to manage user sessions and maintain authentication state.
  • hCaptcha Data: When you interact with hCaptcha on our site, hCaptcha collects certain information to determine if you are a human. This data is processed according to hCaptcha's privacy policy. We do not directly store or process the hCaptcha verification data beyond receiving a successful verification token.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for various purposes:

  • To Provide and Maintain the Service: This includes managing your account, providing email verification services, and ensuring the Service functions correctly.
  • To Personalize Your Experience: To display your display name and profile picture in your dashboard.
  • To Send Verification Emails: We use the to_address, reply_to, app_name, and redirect_url provided via the webhook to construct and send verification emails to your users.
  • To Improve Our Service: We use usage data to understand how our Service is used and to identify areas for improvement.
  • For Security and Fraud Prevention: To protect the integrity and security of our Service and to prevent unauthorized access or fraudulent activities, including using hCaptcha to mitigate bot activity.
  • To Communicate with You: To respond to your inquiries, send you service-related announcements, and provide support.
  • For Internal Analytics: To perform data analysis and research to understand user behavior and trends.

3. How We Share Your Information

We may share your information in the following situations:

  • With Service Providers: We may employ third-party companies and individuals to facilitate our Service ("Service Providers"), to provide the Service on our behalf, to perform Service-related services, or to assist us in analyzing how our Service is used. These third parties have access to your Personal Data only to perform these tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.
    • Firebase: We use Google Firebase for authentication, database (Firestore), and potentially other backend services. Firebase's handling of data is governed by Google's privacy policy.
    • hCaptcha: We integrate hCaptcha for bot detection. Data collected by hCaptcha is subject to hCaptcha's privacy policy.
    • Email Sending Service: The to_address, reply_to, app_name, and redirect_url are sent to our email sending service provider (e.g., Pipedream, or another service that the webhook URL points to) solely for the purpose of sending the verification email.
  • For Legal Reasons: We may disclose your Personal Data in the good faith belief that such action is necessary to:
    • Comply with a legal obligation
    • Protect and defend the rights or property of VerifyFlow
    • Prevent or investigate possible wrongdoing in connection with the Service
    • Protect the personal safety of users of the Service or the public
    • Protect against legal liability
  • Business Transfers: If VerifyFlow is involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your Personal Data may be transferred. We will provide notice before your Personal Data is transferred and becomes subject to a different Privacy Policy.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Data Retention

We retain your Personal Data only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy. We will retain and use your Personal Data to the extent necessary to comply with our legal obligations (for example, if we are required to retain your data to comply with applicable laws), resolve disputes, and enforce our legal agreements and policies.

We will also retain Usage Data for internal analysis purposes. Usage Data is generally retained for a shorter period, except when this data is used to strengthen the security or to improve the functionality of our Service, or we are legally obligated to retain this data for longer periods.
